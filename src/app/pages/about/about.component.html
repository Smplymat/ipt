Our Story

From a Small Oven
to Your Doorstep

Knead to Know began in a tiny home kitchen in 2012, when founder Maria Cruz started baking sourdough loaves for her neighbours. Word spread fast — the warm smell of fresh bread and the honesty of simple, quality ingredients resonated with everyone who tried it.

Today, our full production bakery serves hundreds of families across the city. We still use the same recipes, the same care, and the same belief that great bread makes life a little sweeter.
